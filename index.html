Artist Statement

Our 2026 Creek Show proposal celebrates the life cycle of Datura wrightii (Sacred Datura or Moon Flower), a herbaceous perennial native to Texas and the Edwards Plateau. Central to the cultural traditions of Indigenous peoples of Texas and uniquely adapted to the region’s climate, landscape, and wildlife, it is a charismatic Texas native flora whose story deserves to be told. We propose an immersive installation that reveals its extraordinary botanical beauty through fluid 3D animations depicting an abstracted annual life cycle, highlighting its sculptural stems, buds, flowers, and seed pods. Projected onto the Red River Street bridge and accompanied by glowing dynamic flowers, the installation introduces visitors to this iconic native plant through a memorable nighttime experience.
